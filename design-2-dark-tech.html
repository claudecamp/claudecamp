Sasha & Ying — Pai, Thailand
Your hosts

Sasha & Ying

"I ran a Workaway mountain farm in Pai for years — gardening mornings, hot springs afternoons. Then Claude Code happened. I started building real software here, between watering tomatoes and cutting bamboo. Claude Camp is what happens when we make that intentional."
— Sasha, founder

Ying and I have hosted hundreds of people at this property over the years — through Workaway, through friends of friends, through word of mouth. We built something that felt genuinely restorative, not just beautiful. When AI tools changed what I was able to build here, I wanted to share that feeling: the moment when the mountain air and the right tools collide, and something real gets made.

Ying handles the land, the food, and the warmth that holds everything together. I handle the code sessions, the curriculum, and staying up too late with you debugging whatever broke before bed. We both read every application, and we keep the cohort at 7 so we can actually know everyone.
