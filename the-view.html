FAQ

Common questions

What is The View in Pai?

An open-air bar and workspace directly across from Pai Canyon. No walls — every seat has a panoramic canyon and mountain view. Coffee and breakfast from 8am, cold drinks and cocktails from the afternoon.

Is The View good for remote work?

It's designed for it. Fast WiFi, power at every table, and no city noise. The canyon view turns out to be good for focus — some people don't look up for hours.

What are the opening hours?

Open daily from 8am. Coffee and breakfast from opening; the bar from the afternoon into the evening. WhatsApp us if you're coming late and want to confirm.

Where is The View?

Hot Springs Road, outskirts of Pai — directly across from Pai Canyon. Natural hot springs are across the street. WhatsApp +66922864775 for directions.

Is this part of Claude Camp?

Yes — The View is the open-air bar and workspace on the Claude Camp farm. Claude Camp is a 7-day AI coding bootcamp in Pai. The View is open to the public independently — no need to be a camp participant.
