04 — Sasha & Ying

The farm was
always the plan.
Claude Code was
the surprise.

"I ran a Workaway mountain farm in Pai for years — gardening mornings, hot springs afternoons. Then Claude Code happened. I started building real software here, between watering tomatoes and cutting bamboo. Claude Camp is what happens when we make that intentional."

— Sasha

Sasha and Ying have been hosting travelers at their mountain property in Pai for years. What started as a Workaway — guests helping with the garden, the animals, the daily rhythm of a working farm — became something rarer: a place people didn't want to leave.

85 five-star reviews. A "Top Host" badge. A "Pioneer" badge from a platform that awards it to fewer than 1% of hosts. The guests come back. Some come every year.

Ying runs the kitchen. Northern Thai food — khao soi, laab, fresh herbs from the garden. She's not a background character here. She's the reason the food is what it is, and the reason the communal table feels the way it does.

Then Claude Code happened. Sasha, building software between chores, realized something: the focus, the rhythm, the hot springs at 4pm — these weren't incidental to the creative work. They were the creative work. The farm was, accidentally, the perfect software development environment.

Claude Camp is making that intentional. Seven people. One week. A pace that works.
