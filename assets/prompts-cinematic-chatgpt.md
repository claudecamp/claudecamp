---

## Tips for DALL-E 3 — Cinematic & Atmospheric Images

1. **Be literal about color grading.** DALL-E 3 does not understand shorthand like "teal and orange grade." Describe it explicitly: "warm amber highlights, cool teal-green shadows, rich contrast." The more specific the language, the closer it lands.
2. **Use reference aesthetics, not camera gear.** Instead of camera brand names (which confuse DALL-E), anchor the mood to directors or genres: "Terrence Malick aesthetic," "in the style of a luxury travel documentary," "National Geographic photography." These phrases reliably pull a cinematic quality.
3. **Run each prompt multiple times and compare.** DALL-E 3 has meaningful variation between generations. For community/candid shots especially, run 3–5 times and pick the frame that feels most natural. Adding "candid moment, natural expressions, not posed" helps push away stock-photo stiffness.

## 05 — Community Shot (Group at Night)

**Filename:** `community-night-table.jpg`

**Used in:** The social proof / community section — shows the human side of the retreat. Warm, candid, laughter-filled. Counters the solitude of the other images with energy and connection.

**Alt text:** `A small group of people laughing and talking around a wooden table at night on an open-air bamboo deck, warm lantern light, tropical jungle surrounding them`

**Prompt:**
```
A candid documentary photograph of a group of five people — a mixed international group including a Western man and a Thai woman — seated around a low teak wooden table on an open-air bamboo deck at night in a tropical jungle setting in northern Thailand. Candid moment, natural expressions, not posed. They are laughing and in deep conversation. Laptops and notebooks are open among shared dishes of Thai food and glasses. Warm amber tungsten lantern light and string lights overhead cast rich golden pools of light. The surrounding jungle is dark and textured beyond the deck's edge. Fireflies are softly visible in the dark background. Motion blur on gesturing hands suggests real spontaneous movement. Shallow depth of field, faces lit with genuine warmth. One person is pointing at a laptop screen while others lean in. Cinematic vérité documentary style, in the style of a documentary about a creative commune. Color graded with fire-warm amber highlights and cool teal-green shadows in the dark jungle beyond. Visible analog film grain. No watermarks, no logos, no text.
```

> **Tip:** Run this prompt 3–5 times and select the most natural frame. If expressions feel too stiff or staged, add "unguarded candid moment, imperfect, real" to the prompt. DALL-E 3 responds well to explicit direction about naturalness here.

## Usage Notes

- All hero and section backgrounds should be generated in **wide cinematic landscape format** (16:9 feel) and upscaled to at least 2560px wide before use
- Editorial stills should be generated in **horizontal editorial format** (3:2 feel) — export at 1800px wide minimum
- Run each prompt 4x and select the frame that reads best through the CSS gradient overlays (test by overlaying the gradient stack in Figma or the browser before committing)
- The laptop screen in prompt 03 should be blurred or darkened in post if any legible UI appears — keep it atmospheric, not product-literal
- For prompt 05, run multiple times and compare — variation is the feature here. Specify "candid moment, natural expressions, not posed" and add "imperfect, real" if results feel stock-photo stiff
